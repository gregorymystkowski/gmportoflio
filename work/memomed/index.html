01 / CONTEXT

Activation is a journey, not a single switch.

A practice needs to set up its account, connect Google Calendar, configure reminders, activate WhatsApp and choose a subscription. The design challenge is to make these connected steps understandable while keeping the purpose of each integration clear.

02 / DESIGN SCOPE

Make the next step feel manageable.

My scope covered the end-to-end onboarding journey, the product offer, subscription flows and the marketing website. The flow below organizes those responsibilities into a portfolio illustration; it is not a screenshot of the live interface.

03 / ITERATION

Connect the promise to the product.

I used GA4 and Google Ads data to examine landing-page engagement and demo conversion, then iterated messaging and UX. Real-user feedback informed the product at a high level. No specific testing protocol or measured conversion lift is claimed here.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

An active product, still evolving.

MemoMed serves multiple active practices. My work combines product decisions and UX with AI-assisted technical delivery on FastAPI, Google Cloud and Firestore. The ongoing focus is the journey from signup to activation.

01 / CONTEXT

Activation is a journey, not a single switch.

A practice needs to understand what MemoMed does before connecting its calendar and activating reminders. The public website explains Google Calendar integration, WhatsApp reminders and periodic recalls. Its primary trial calls to action lead to a consultation booking, rather than a self-service signup screen.

02 / DESIGN SCOPE

Explain the service before asking for commitment.

My scope covered practice onboarding, calendar connection, reminder configuration, WhatsApp activation and subscriptions, alongside the marketing website. The screenshots here show the actual public website: its service explanation and calendar-to-reminder journey. They do not show the private application or verify its onboarding screens.

03 / ITERATION

Connect the promise to the product.

I used GA4 and Google Ads data to examine landing-page engagement and demo conversion, then iterated messaging and UX. Real-user feedback informed the product at a high level. No specific testing protocol or measured conversion lift is claimed here.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

An active product, still evolving.

MemoMed serves multiple active practices. My work combines product decisions and UX with AI-assisted technical delivery on FastAPI, Google Cloud and Firestore. The ongoing focus is the journey from signup to activation.

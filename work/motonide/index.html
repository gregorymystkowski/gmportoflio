01 / CONTEXT

Before a rider books, they need to understand.

Booking a motorcycle involves more than finding the right bike. Rental conditions, costs, verification, payment and deposits all shape confidence. Owners also need a clear way to list a motorcycle and understand how the marketplace works.

02 / DESIGN SCOPE

Treat trust as part of the journey.

I designed core rider and owner journeys and coordinated technical and commercial partners. The central tradeoff was balancing usability with verification, deposits, dispute handling and marketplace economics. This illustrative flow highlights the rider experience and the information needed before committing.

03 / ITERATION

Learn from running the whole product.

Early marketplace activity and user feedback informed priorities. Operating the venture put UX decisions alongside commercial and operational constraints, including payments, deposits and disputes. No rental volume, revenue or conversion result is claimed.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

800 people. 150 motorcycles. Real responsibilities.

The venture grew to 800 registered users and 150 listed motorcycles. It gave me direct experience owning a product beyond its interface: coordinating delivery, making tradeoffs and supporting the experience around the booking.

MOTONIDE / PROJECT · MARKETPLACE

A little less doubt. A lot more open road.

I worked on Motonide from its first version through launch and ongoing use, focusing on the search and rental journeys. The marketplace reached 1,000+ registered users and 300+ motorcycle listings. The screens shown here are from the current site, which focuses on discovery, comparison and requests to professional rental operators.

01 / CONTEXT

Before a rider sends a request, they need clarity.

The linked Motonide site helps riders explore motorcycles from professional rental operators. It separates the daily rate, included mileage and deposit, and identifies details that still need confirmation. The listing makes clear that an availability request is not a payment or a confirmed booking.

02 / DESIGN SCOPE

Give the rider the information to ask better questions.

The screens below come directly from the linked Motonide implementation: the discovery page, catalog and motorcycle detail. Its current flow ends in an availability request and direct coordination with the rental operator. Earlier stages of the project also involved bookings, payments, deposits and disputes. The current site shown here does not process those payments.

03 / ITERATION

Learn from running the whole product.

Early marketplace activity and user feedback helped me decide what to revisit. Working on practical aspects of the service, including payments, deposits and disputes, gave me useful context for interface decisions.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

What I learned from the project.

Motonide reached 1,000+ registered users and 300+ motorcycle listings. Working on it gave me experience following a product over time: coordinating development, choosing priorities and supporting the experience around the booking.
